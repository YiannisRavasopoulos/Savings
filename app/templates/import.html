What the file should look like

A header row naming the columns, then one expense per row. Commas, semicolons or tabs all work.

date,description,category,amount,notes
2026-10-03,Groceries,Food,54.20,
05/10/2026,Train ticket,Transport,"12,50",work trip
  • date and amount are required. Dates: 2026-10-05 or day first (05/10/2026, 05.10.2026). Amounts: 12.50, 12,50, €1.234,56.
  • description, category, notes and one_off (yes/1) are optional. Other columns are ignored, and common names like payee, memo or value are recognised.
  • Bank exports: if some amounts are negative, the negative ones are taken as spending and positive ones (money in) are skipped.
  • This app's own export (Download my data → expenses.csv) can be imported too. Rows someone else paid are skipped.
  • Rows that match an expense you already have (same date, amount and description) are flagged and skipped unless you choose otherwise.
  • Up to {{ max_rows }} rows and 2 MB per file.
