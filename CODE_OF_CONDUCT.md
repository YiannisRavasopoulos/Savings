# Code of conduct

Savings is a small project made for people managing their own money. Keep it a friendly place.

**Do:**

- be kind and patient, especially with people new to self-hosting or to code;
- assume good intent, and give feedback about the work, not the person;
- keep discussions on topic and help others get unstuck.

**Don't:**

- harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason;
- post someone else's personal or financial information;
- share real spending data, tokens or passwords in issues, even your own.

**If something goes wrong:** report it privately to the maintainer (through the repository's
private reporting on the **Security** tab, or by contacting them on GitHub). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people
who don't follow this code.
